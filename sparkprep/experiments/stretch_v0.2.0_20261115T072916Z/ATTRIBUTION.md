# Data, model and source attribution

Natural sequences and per-accession provenance: UniProt Consortium, UniProtKB, retrieved 2026-11-14 in the frozen preparation. Accessions, organism/strain provenance and individual source URLs are retained in inputs/sequence_metadata.tsv. Sequence SHA256 values are preserved. UniProt database copyrightable content is distributed under CC BY 4.0: https://www.uniprot.org/help/license and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. The retrieved official license record is provenance/uniprot_license.json. Data are copied without sequence edits; new alignment mapping, sensitivity and model scores are derived analyses. See https://www.uniprot.org/help/publications for citation guidance.

Experimental structures are existing deposited coordinates, not new predictions: PDB 1GGQ (B31) https://doi.org/10.2210/pdb1GGQ/pdb and PDB 1G5Z (N40) https://doi.org/10.2210/pdb1G5Z/pdb. Attribution and original citations are embedded in the unmodified input mmCIF files. PDB archive data are openly redistributable under the wwPDB data-use policy; no RCSB website images, third-party article full text or third-party viewer code are included here. https://www.wwpdb.org/about/usage-policies and https://www.rcsb.org/pages/policies . Biological assemblies, not arbitrary asymmetric-unit chain pairs, define the contact computations.
