Observed asymmetry. Unresolved cause.

During this trial, Spark29 reported a 507 MHz SM clock; Spark30 reported 2,509–2,535 MHz. Runtime versions matched. These observations do not establish why the clocks differed or prove the cause of the earlier slowdown. No power, driver or host settings were changed.

Post-hoc partition selection; one timing observation; diagnostic sampling only in the exploratory run; no independently calibrated host clocks. No general scaling, NCCL/RDMA or VAST-throughput claim.

The biology remains the reason to compute

Thirty natural OspC sequences connect observed agreement, model predictability and experimental structural evidence. The controlled B11 diagnostic was negative. These results do not establish protective immunity, accessibility or clinical utility.
